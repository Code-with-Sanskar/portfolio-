Process

How a project runs.

Six steps, in the same order for UI/UX, graphic and video. Only the artefacts change.

UI/UXGraphicVideo
  1. 01

    Brief

    What it has to do, who it is for, where it will live.

    UI/UX
    Product goal, users, platform
    Graphic
    Message, brand, formats
    Video
    Story, channel, runtime
  2. 02

    Research

    Look before drawing anything.

    UI/UX
    Competitors, user interviews
    Graphic
    References, market, mood
    Video
    References, footage, music
  3. 03

    Concept

    One direction, rough on purpose, agreed before any polish.

    UI/UX
    User flows, wireframes
    Graphic
    Sketches, moodboard
    Video
    Script, storyboard
  4. 04

    Make

    The long middle: every screen, every artwork, every cut.

    UI/UX
    Hi-fi screens, components
    Graphic
    Final artwork, typography
    Video
    Edit, motion, sound
  5. 05

    Review

    Put it in front of people and fix what breaks.

    UI/UX
    Prototype, usability test
    Graphic
    Client rounds, print proof
    Video
    Review cuts, notes
  6. 06

    Deliver

    Files the next person can open and use.

    UI/UX
    Specs, tokens, handoff
    Graphic
    Print and digital files
    Video
    Exports for every channel
